# ✨ Lume & Luxe | Curated Decor, Architectural Inspiration & Room Guides for Modern Homes

A premier British luxury interior platform combining a **High-Standard E-Commerce Store** with an **Independent Architectural Design Journal & Room Playbook Directory**. Designed in the prestigious editorial ethos of **[The Spruce](https://www.thespruce.com)**, written strictly in **British English (UK)**, and styled with a timeless **Quiet Luxury** aesthetic.
