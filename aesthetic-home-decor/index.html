The Story of Lume & Luxe

Born from a passionate reverence for British architectural proportion and Scandinavian organic warmth, Lume & Luxe bridges timeless interior inspiration with masterfully crafted decor.

Our Core Philosophy

Homes Formed with Spatial Balance, Tactile Honesty & Lasting Calm

We believe an extraordinary home is never assembled haphazardly. It requires an understanding of daylight orientation, noble materials like honed travertine and quarter-sawn English oak, and purposeful negative space that allows rooms to breathe.

Our atelier brings together architectural writers, lighting specialists, and master craftsmen who create both our detailed room-by-room playbooks and our limited-edition decor pieces.

01.

Architectural Rigour

Every piece and playbook is designed according to golden-ratio proportions and true residential scale.

02.

Bespoke Materiality

We reject synthetic composites in favour of solid timbers, natural unbleached wools, and honest patinating metals.

03.

Quiet Luxury

Zero loud logos or fleeting trends. Our palette is calibrated to harmonise with daylight and soft candlelight.
